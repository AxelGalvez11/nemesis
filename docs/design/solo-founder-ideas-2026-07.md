# Solo-Founder Web-App Ideas — 2026 (post-PharmaOrb)

> Deep research, 5 web angles + synthesis. **Core finding: a good solo idea = OWNED DISTRIBUTION × a WALLET-HOLDING now-payer, not artifact type.** The founder's one real asset is the Pharma Bro channel; the winning move is to point it at brands/creators (NOT free viewers = the PharmaOrb repeat) and reuse the existing evidence engine.

## Executive summary

A good solo idea in 2026 is decided by two gates, not by artifact type: OWNED DISTRIBUTION (can you reach 1,000+ target buyers this week for $0?) x a WALLET-HOLDING NOW-PAYER (someone with a budget line who pays in month 1). All five research angles converge on this, and ~54% of indie products make $0 precisely because they miss one gate. Your one real asset is your health/science channel — so the winning move is: keep the channel as the distribution engine, but point the product at a payer who is NOT your free viewers (that is the literal PharmaOrb repeat). The payers with budgets adjacent to a debunk audience are (a) supplement/wellness BRANDS ($180B market, urgent FTC/FDA claim-compliance pain, $99-499/mo WTP) and (b) OTHER health/science CREATORS ($29-99/mo, and you'd use the tool yourself — passes the founder-would-use-it test hardest). Headline picks, best-first: a brand-facing claims/evidence-compliance tool (highest ceiling, reuses your PharmaOrb evidence engine) and a creator 'debunk-in-a-box' tool (fastest yes). Below those: a teach-the-skill cohort with the app as the front-door, an affiliate-native supplement finder (payer = brand, not viewer), an embeddable evidence-badge widget, a free BS-detector lead magnet, and — since you asked about games — one honest game: a daily 'Fact or Cap' health-myth guessing game (Wordle/GeoGuessr-shaped, subscription or brand-sponsored), ranked below the B2B tools. Hard avoids: ad-monetized .io/hypercasual/idle games on Poki (rents someone else's audience, earns pennies), AI-generated games (~53% sales penalty + stigma), any consumer health SaaS sold to your viewers (PharmaOrb #2 — and SuppleMate already ships the bare version), and directories/programmatic-SEO as a core (year-two revenue, fails the 6-month self-funding test). You do not yet know your channel's email-capture rate, which is the single variable that sets every revenue number — so validate by pre-selling before writing code.

## Ranked, scored shortlist (best-first)

### 1. BRAND-FACING CLAIMS/EVIDENCE COMPLIANCE TOOL: supplement/DTC-wellness brands paste ad copy or a label, get flagged risky FTC/FDA structure-function claims + a cited evidence pack backing the defensible ones + a shareable 'evidence receipt' card. Reuses the PharmaOrb evidence engine as the answer key.
- **Who pays:** Supplement/DTC-wellness brands and their agencies, $99-499/mo. A budget line + urgent fear-of-fine pain = high WTP, low churn (painkiller, not vitamin).
- **Distribution:** Your Pharma Bro channel is a lead-gen magnet: post 'brands that got this claim wrong' shorts; cold-DM the exact brands you've covered (or ones who want to NOT get debunked). You already talk to this buyer on camera.
- **Build:** Medium — v1 = paste-copy -> flag-claims -> cite. Largely a re-skin of the existing PharmaOrb evidence engine you already built.
- **Time to revenue:** If the channel converts at even a low rate, first paid pilots in 4-8 weeks via cold-DM; can hit ~$5k MRR (10-20 brands) in 3-6 months. Conditioned on your actual reach — unknown until you test.
- **Odds/caveat:** Highest-ceiling, highest-WTP option and reuses your graveyard asset. Soft spot: enterprise-y sales cycle, and you must nail 'defensible vs risky' accuracy. SuppleMate (Apr 2026) validates demand for evidence tools but ships consumer-side — the B2B compliance angle is still open and your distribution + engine is the moat.
- **Cheapest validation:** Cold-DM 10 supplement brands you've already covered: 'I'll audit your top 3 ad claims for evidence + FTC risk, $X.' Collect 2-3 paid pilots or deposits THIS WEEK before building anything.

### 2. CREATOR 'DEBUNK-IN-A-BOX' TOOL: paste a trending health claim, get back a sourced, script-ready short — pulls citations, names the fallacy, drafts the hook. The exact workflow you do manually now, sold to your peers.
- **Who pays:** Other explainer/debunk/health/science creators at $29-99/mo. A prosumer with sponsorship revenue ($200-9k/post) protecting credibility — a real wallet, not a broke viewer.
- **Distribution:** You are the credentialed proof-of-value. One 'how I make these videos in 20 minutes' post converts; sell peer-to-peer in creator communities. Every output card is watermarked = organic backlink.
- **Build:** Medium — citation retrieval + fallacy tagging + hook drafting. Shares most infrastructure with the brand tool.
- **Time to revenue:** Fastest 'yes' of any option because you dogfood it publicly; first subs within weeks of a single demo video. Ceiling lower than the brand tool.
- **Odds/caveat:** Passes the founder-would-use-it test hardest (you need it yourself). Lower WTP and a smaller TAM than brands. Strongest when paired with the brand tool as a two-audience wedge off one codebase.
- **Cheapest validation:** Post one 'here's the tool I use to make these' short with a waitlist link + DM 15 creators in your niche offering lifetime early-access at a deposit price (Subscribr pre-sold 50 LTDs for $20k pre-code). Count hand-raises in a week.

### 3. TEACH-THE-SKILL COHORT CHALLENGE ('Spot Health BS in 7 Days' / 'How to Read a Study'), with a web app as the challenge platform (daily lessons + a study-scoring worksheet). The app is the front-door, not a standalone SaaS.
- **Who pays:** Your fans, $97-297 one-time per cohort. This is the ONE thing fans WILL pay for — the debunking SKILL they already value and can't get elsewhere — not a utility.
- **Distribution:** Run it live off a viral debunk; every video CTA drives signups. Paid challenges are the highest-converting creator format (70-80% completion, 22-35% conversion of engaged free members).
- **Build:** Low-Medium — the 'app' is a lightweight cohort platform; most value is your curriculum + live sessions.
- **Time to revenue:** Can bank revenue in a single launch weekend to an existing list. Fastest cash of any option.
- **Odds/caveat:** High-converting and survives the PharmaOrb test (fans buy a skill, not a health tool). Caveat: cohorts are time/energy-heavy for a solo founder and are not recurring unless productized into a community rung.
- **Cheapest validation:** Pin a 'I'm running a paid 7-day Spot-Health-BS cohort, comment to pre-order at $97' CTA on one video. If your own audience won't pre-order what they watch free, no app saves it — same test PharmaOrb failed, for $0.

### 4. AFFILIATE-NATIVE 'EVIDENCE-CHECKED SUPPLEMENT FINDER': a consumer web tool ranking supplements by evidence quality that routes buyers to vetted products via 25-40% recurring affiliate commissions. Payer = the brand's affiliate payout, NOT the viewer.
- **Who pays:** Supplement brands via affiliate commission (25-40% recurring on subscribe-and-save). The viewer clicks free; the brand pays you.
- **Distribution:** Every debunk ends 'here's the evidence-ranked alternative' link. Your content IS the demo.
- **Build:** Low-Medium — ranking + affiliate link routing over your evidence data.
- **Time to revenue:** Revenue trickles from day one but scales with traffic volume; meaningful money needs sustained channel pushes.
- **Odds/caveat:** Sidesteps the 'fans won't pay' trap by monetizing brands. But SuppleMate/Remedy (Apr 2026) already ships a consumer ingredient-checker, so your ONLY moat is distribution + the affiliate twist — do NOT build this as a paid subscription, and treat affiliate as a secondary layer (called out as declining/low-stability in 2026).
- **Cheapest validation:** Add one affiliate 'evidence-ranked pick' link to your next 3 videos and measure click-through + conversion before building any ranking UI.

### 5. EMBEDDABLE EVIDENCE-BADGE WIDGET: a 'cited-claim / evidence receipt' widget health blogs, supplement product pages, and creator link-in-bios embed to show sourced backing. Every embed = a backlink + a paying customer.
- **Who pays:** Site owners + DTC health brands at $29/mo (matches testimonial-wall widget benchmark); usage tiers push net revenue retention to 115-130%.
- **Distribution:** 'Install the widget I use' CTA on every video, plus each embed is a free placement + backlink = compounding organic reach.
- **Build:** Low — a widget/embed + billing via a merchant-of-record (ExtensionPay/Fungies/Paddle).
- **Time to revenue:** Slower than an audience push (distribution compounds over months) but low support burden.
- **Odds/caveat:** Passes both gates and is cheap to maintain, but modest ceiling ($2-5k MRR range) and slower distribution than a direct channel push. Best as a bolt-on to the brand tool, not a standalone bet.
- **Cheapest validation:** Mock the badge, put it on your own site/link-in-bio, and DM 10 health bloggers/brands: 'want this evidence badge on your product page for $29/mo?' Count yeses.

### 6. FREE 'BS DETECTOR' WEB TOOL AS A LEAD MAGNET (not the product): paste a health claim/supplement label/headline, get a plain-English red-flag breakdown engineered to go viral back into the channel and capture emails.
- **Who pays:** Nobody directly — this is top-of-funnel. It monetizes via whatever paid product (brand tool, cohort, community) wins validation behind it.
- **Distribution:** The tool's outputs ARE short-form content; every output is shareable and points back to your channel + email list. This is the piece the ad-aggregator game model can never replicate.
- **Build:** Low — a single-page tool over your evidence engine.
- **Time to revenue:** $0 on its own; its job is to convert rented short-form reach into an OWNED email list, which is the prerequisite for every other idea's revenue.
- **Odds/caveat:** As a PAID product this is PharmaOrb #2 — do NOT charge for it. Its only viable role is the free front-door that fixes your leakiest problem (no email capture).
- **Cheapest validation:** Ship a one-page version, put it behind an email gate, drive one video to it, and measure the email-capture rate — the single number that decides every other revenue estimate here.

### 7. DAILY 'FACT OR CAP' HEALTH-MYTH GUESSING GAME (the one honest game): a once-a-day Wordle/GeoGuessr-shaped web game where players judge a real health/supplement/drug claim, get the evidence verdict, and share a streak score. Answer key = your PharmaOrb evidence engine.
- **Who pays:** Freemium subscribers for archive/hard-mode/no-ads (GeoGuessr is the ONLY proven wallet-holding web-game buyer), and later supplement/health brands for a sponsored weekly round.
- **Distribution:** End every short with 'today's claim is live — beat the streak?', pointing your OWNED audience at your OWNED game (never Poki). Shareable daily result cards drive virality back into the channel.
- **Build:** Low-Medium — a daily-content web game over your existing claim/verdict data.
- **Time to revenue:** Subscriptions ramp slowly; brand-sponsored rounds are the faster money once you have daily-return numbers.
- **Odds/caveat:** The ONLY game shape that clears both gates (owned distribution + a subscription/brand payer). But there is NO verifiable fresh 2026 solo web-game millionaire, GeoGuessr is VC-scaled not a solo comp, and don't abruptly paywall the fun (GeoGuessr's Steam sub-gate got 'overwhelmingly negative' reviews). Ranked below the B2B tools on purpose.
- **Cheapest validation:** Post a manual daily 'Fact or Cap' round as plain shorts for two weeks and measure whether the audience clicks through and RETURNS daily — before writing any game code.

### 8. NON-HEALTH BORING B2B ESCAPE HATCH: a vertical $50-500/mo workflow tool (dunning/payment-recovery, niche scheduling/booking, compliance) for a business type with a budget line, using the channel only as build-in-public distribution.
- **Who pays:** A specific business niche with a budget (best WTP + lowest churn of any buyer type). Anchors: kitchen-appliance-store CRM hit $6.7k MRR/89 customers at $75/mo in 6 months.
- **Distribution:** COLD for this founder — you'd cold-start a new audience and do B2B outreach, forfeiting your one asset. Build-in-public content warms it slightly but you are not the buyer.
- **Build:** Low-Medium — CRUD + one integration; solo-buildable in <6 weeks.
- **Time to revenue:** 8-12 weeks to ~10 customers/$5k MRR IF you'll grind cold outreach.
- **Odds/caveat:** Best ABSTRACT base rates, but ranked LAST for THIS founder because it throws away the channel — the exact distribution advantage most $0 founders spend 18 months failing to build. Only pursue if you genuinely want out of health and will do cold sales.
- **Cheapest validation:** Pick one niche, put up a one-page offer, cold-DM/email 20 target businesses, and collect 5 deposits before building. If they won't commit pre-build, kill it.

## Top picks

- LEVERAGE THE CHANNEL, DON'T GO FRESH — but route the product to BRANDS/CREATORS, not your viewers. Going fresh forfeits your only asset; selling to viewers rebuilds PharmaOrb. This resolution is decisive, not a maybe: the channel is the distribution engine, the wallet belongs to someone else.
- #1 is a two-horse race, decided by real deposits this week, not a coin flip: the BRAND claims-compliance tool (higher ceiling $99-499/mo, reuses your PharmaOrb engine, but slower trust + cold-DM sales) vs the CREATOR debunk-in-a-box tool (faster yes, $29-99/mo, you'd use it yourself). Run BOTH $0 pre-sells in parallel and let paying deposits pick the winner — same codebase serves both, so you lose nothing by testing both.
- If you want a game, build ONLY the daily 'Fact or Cap' myth game (subscription or brand-sponsored, seeded by your channel) and treat it as a top-of-funnel/retention loop, not the revenue bet. Rank it below the B2B tools. Never ship an ad-monetized .io/hypercasual/idle game on Poki — it rents someone else's audience and neutralizes your one edge.

## Avoid

- Ad-monetized web games on Poki/CrazyGames (.io, hypercasual, idle): $500-3k/mo ceiling, spike-then-die, and you RENT the platform's audience — the worst possible fit because it neutralizes your channel. ~70% of solo devs never profit; median indie game earns ~$249-350 lifetime.
- AI-generated games: ~53% worse Steam sales, measurable stigma (84.6% vs 88.3% median review), market flooding (8x jump in AI-flagged games), and only ~18% odds any clears $1M.
- A consumer health-research/ingredient SaaS sold to your viewers = PharmaOrb #2. Wrong payer (free-content viewers won't buy health software), and SuppleMate/Remedy (Apr 2026) already ships the bare consumer version.
- Charging for the free BS-detector tool — it only works as a lead magnet; as a paid product it repeats the vitamin/no-payer trap.
- Niche directories or programmatic-SEO as the CORE business: distribution-rich but wallet-poor, 'consistent revenue in year two,' ad-network pennies — fails the 6-month self-funding test. Fine only as a bolt-on funnel to a paying core.
- Meme apps / memecoins / any virality-or-die play: 54% of meme apps make $0; memecoins graduate at 0.26%. Survivorship-bias lotteries.
- Thin AI wrappers with no proprietary data: ~90% fail, 25-35% margins, ~65% churn, no moat.
- Treating promotional/survivorship numbers as forecasts: Poki's €100k/dev + 10x, the '$77k/35-apps' viral claim, and third-party GeoGuessr revenue are soft — anchor on the failure medians instead.

## The meta-lesson (must carry from PharmaOrb)

Pick the PAYER before the product, and make sure the payer is NOT the same free audience that watches you for entertainment — then prove it by pre-selling to a named wallet-holder BEFORE writing a line of code. PharmaOrb failed because it had no owned distribution AND its only conceivable buyer was the broke viewers who watch free debunks (it failed the 'founder wouldn't even use it' test). This time you HAVE distribution (the channel), so the only remaining discipline is to aim it at a wallet with a budget line — supplement brands or fellow creators — and get a real deposit before you build.

## Recommendation (this week, before code)

This week, before writing any code, run TWO $0 pre-sell tests in parallel and let real money decide: (1) BRAND test — cold-DM 10 supplement brands you've already covered on the channel: 'I'll audit your top 3 ad claims for evidence + FTC risk for $X,' aiming for 2-3 paid pilots or deposits. (2) CREATOR test — post one 'here's the tool I use to make these videos' short with a waitlist link and DM 15 peer creators offering lifetime early-access at a deposit price. In parallel, ship a one-page free BS-detector behind an email gate and drive one video to it purely to measure your email-capture rate — the single unknown that sets every revenue estimate. Whichever pre-sell collects real deposits first becomes your #1; both are served by the same PharmaOrb evidence engine, so you build once. If neither raises a paying hand in a week, do NOT build — that's the same signal PharmaOrb ignored, caught this time for free. Treat the daily 'Fact or Cap' game as an optional retention/funnel layer to validate later with two weeks of manual shorts, never as the money bet.
