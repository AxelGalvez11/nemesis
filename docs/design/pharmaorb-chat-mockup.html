Metformin at eGFR 40 mL/min/1.73m²

At an eGFR of 40, metformin is not contraindicated but requires caution: per the FDA label, initiation is not recommended below 45, and the dose should be reviewed — many clinicians cap total daily dose around 1000 mg in this range. FDA 1 Discontinue if eGFR falls below 30. FDA 2 Recent real-world data support continued use in stage 3b CKD with monitoring. PMID 3
