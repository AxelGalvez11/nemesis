# xai: observed interaction patterns


**Display type gets its presence from size and tightness, not from weight.** 36px at weight 400
with `-0.72px`, and the 60px hero at weight 500 with `-1.5px` tracking: the weight barely moves
while the tracking triples. Adopted as our display treatment and encoded in the type tokens.

**Monospace as a labelling device.** Small mono at positive tracking marks technical metadata and
separates it from prose without a colour, a border or an icon. Adopted for source metadata.

**An interface with no accent at all**, proving the accent is optional and that size, weight and
alpha are sufficient for hierarchy.

**One transition for the whole site** (`0.15s`). Noted and deliberately **not** adopted: see
`REFERENCE_CONFLICTS.md` §6.
