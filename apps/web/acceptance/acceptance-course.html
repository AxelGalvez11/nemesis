Key Terms

Learn these three terms and what each one does.

TermDefinition
PhotosynthesisConversion of light energy into chemical energy stored as sugar.
ChlorophyllPigment that absorbs light, mostly in the blue and red wavelengths.
StomataPores in a leaf surface that let carbon dioxide in and water vapour out.

Cause and Effect

Light intensity

Increasing light intensity increases the rate of photosynthesis until another factor becomes limiting. Once carbon dioxide or temperature becomes the constraint, adding more light produces no further increase.
